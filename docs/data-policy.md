# Data and publication policy

The repository includes research code, configuration, schemas, bibliographic references, source/model hashes, aggregate metrics and original analytical figures.

The following remain local and excluded from Git:

- MASCO PDF, extracted occupation inventory, descriptions, tasks and page evidence.
- ESCO source archives and the full derived occupation tables.
- Model weights, package environments, vector caches and score databases.
- Complete candidate rows, blinded reviewer exports, reviewer identities, decisions and accepted-mapping drafts.
- Prior project artifacts, personal filesystem paths and machine-specific logs.

The MASCO publication's reproduction and redistribution permissions remain unresolved. Local research access does not establish permission to redistribute the PDF or its full extracted text. ESCO and model materials retain their own terms. Jina v5 model weights are marked CC-BY-NC-4.0; the other model terms are documented in source references. No model weights are included here.

Before adding any new file, inspect its contents as well as its extension. Git ignore rules are a convenience, not a content review. Do not use force-add to bypass the exclusions without a documented reason and the necessary rights.

Aggregate candidate counts are not validated employment statistics. Charts compare research outputs; they do not establish mapping accuracy or workforce prevalence.
