# Validation status

The computational pipeline has completed extraction checks, retrieval, reranking and blinded-pool preparation. The current record contains **zero independent human decisions and zero accepted mappings**.

The review sample contains 330 occupations: 30 calibration cases, 200 probability-test cases and 100 purposive challenge cases. Each major group contributes 33 occupations. Entire unit groups and connected duplicate-title components are assigned together to development or held-out partitions.

Two independent human reviews and a separate adjudication are required for accepted mappings. Reviewers must consider source and target scope, distinguish equivalence from containment and overlap, and search beyond the suggested pool. An occupation may have zero, one or several accepted targets.

The 99.1% figure in the charts is average pairwise agreement between encoders after common reranking. It is not accuracy. Reviewing the 330-source sample could support evaluation under that sampling design; it would not validate every unreviewed occupation in the full inventory.
